One clear next step. Every second, every screen, every crew.

Most tactical apps are a map with a hundred buttons. Fieldlink starts from the person holding the phone: what they can see, what their hands can do, and what happens when the network drops.

Push-to-talk and video

The radio is the app.

Hold the big green button to talk; slide up to lock it hands-free. Everyone hears the talk-permit chirp and the roger beep, sees who has the channel, and gets a written transcript in the log. Switch to video and the same button streams what you see.

Report anything

Long-press. Pick one. Done.

Four large choices open where your thumb already is. Severity is one tap. Position, time and callsign attach themselves. Serious hazards draw a zone on everyone's map, and anyone who walks into it is warned out loud.

Glove-sized targetsHazard zonesUndo, never “Are you sure?”
Man-down

It notices when you stop.

A hard fall or a minute without movement starts a countdown you can't miss. Tap anywhere or press the side button if you're fine. If you don't, SOS goes out with your position and the last five minutes of your track.

Fall detectionNo-movement alarmSlide-to-send SOS5-second cancel
Way out

Your own steps lead you back.

Smoke, darkness, a building you've never seen. Fieldlink turns the track you walked in into a route back to the door, with turn cues you can feel through your glove: one pulse for left, three for right.

Reverse breadcrumbHaptic turn cuesFloor tracking

Dark is a feature.
Night is red.

The default Field mode is a calm graphite that reads in direct sun. Night mode drops to dim red to protect night vision. Blackout turns the screen off entirely and moves every alert to vibration.

Vitals that warn before it's urgent.

Breathing-air pressure, heart rate and gas readings from wearables. Turnaround at 50%, exit at 30%, and the lead sees it at the same moment you do.
